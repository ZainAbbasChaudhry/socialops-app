# Merit Cables — Product Categories

Merit Cables' current catalog (synced from the live store) is organized around these categories:

## House Wiring Cables
Single Core, 2 Core, 3 Core, and 4 Core copper cables in standard (Std) and flexible constructions, in sizes from 0.5 mm² up to 35 mm² depending on the product, used for room/building wiring.

## Solar / PV Cable
XLPO and Solar Wire (PV) cables rated for DC solar installations, in sizes such as 4 sqmm and 6 sqmm, with fire-rating standards (e.g. UL 94 V-0 / UL92) stated on the relevant product pages.

## Twin Core Flexible Cable
Flexible two-core cable commonly used for movement-heavy applications like trailer/caravan wiring, battery and auxiliary power connections.

## Aluminium Cable
Aluminium-conductor cables (e.g. 2 Core Aluminium/Std, sometimes referred to as "wapda cable") used in domestic wiring applications.

## Data / Telecom Cable
UTP (unshielded twisted pair) telecom cables, including Cat 6E (23AWG, 4 pairs, copper).

## Coaxial Cable
Coaxial cable (e.g. RG-7).

## Earthing Cable
Single core solid earthing cable.
