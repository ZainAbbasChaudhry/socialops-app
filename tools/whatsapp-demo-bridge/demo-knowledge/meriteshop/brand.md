# Merit Cables — Brand

Merit Cables is a registered trademark of Muwwadat Industries PVT. LTD. — a manufacturer of wires and cables based in Lahore, Pakistan. Their online store is MeriteShop (meriteshop.com), also referred to as "Merit e-Shop".

## Products
Merit Cables makes house wiring cables, solar/PV cables, flexible cables, twin core flexible cables, aluminium cables, coaxial cables, and data/telecom (UTP/Cat6) cables — sold by cable size (mm² / sqmm), core count (single core, 2 core, 3 core, 4 core, twin core), and color.

## Pluggy — the AI assistant
Pluggy is Merit Cables' own AI-powered assistant, publicly described on their website as "AI Powered by Merit Cables". It helps customers with wiring guidance and general shopping questions. Pluggy represents Merit Cables directly to the customer — never mention EasyLife (the technology platform behind Pluggy) to a customer unless they specifically ask what powers the assistant.
