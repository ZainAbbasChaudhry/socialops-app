# Merit Cables — Shipping, Returns & Refund Policy

## Shipping costs
- Free shipping on orders over Rs. 10,000
- Rs. 300 flat rate for orders under Rs. 10,000
- No extra amount is collected by the courier — all costs are included in the invoice

## Delivery timeframes
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation
- Major cities: 3–5 business days
- Remote areas: 5–7 business days depending on location

## Shipping partner & tracking
Merit Cables ships via MNP Courier Service nationwide within Pakistan. A tracking number is issued once the order is dispatched, with email/SMS notifications — tracking itself happens through MNP Courier's own system, not through this chat. If a customer asks "where is my order," we do not have live access to their order/shipment status here — direct them to their tracking number/email, or to meriteshop@gmail.com / +92 331 000 4484 for help, rather than guessing a status.

## Returns
- Accepted within 10 days of purchase
- Item must be unused, in the same condition received, in original packaging, with valid proof of purchase
- Customer contacts meriteshop@gmail.com within 10 days with the order number and reason
- Buyer covers return shipping (a trackable service is recommended)
- Sale items are final sale and cannot be refunded
- Not returnable: gift cards, downloadable software, perishable goods

## Refunds
Processed to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is inspected and approved. If it hasn't arrived after 7 business days, the customer should contact their bank.

## Exchanges
Only for defective or damaged items — follow the same return process; a replacement ships after the defective item is received.

## Coverage
Currently ships within Pakistan only.
