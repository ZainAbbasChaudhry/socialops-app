# EasyLife AI Sales OS — Product Overview

The EasyLife AI Sales OS is a dashboard that helps a business capture, qualify, and follow up with leads automatically across social media and WhatsApp, so fewer leads go cold waiting for a human to respond.

## Social media lead capture
Comments and DMs on a business's connected Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, and YouTube accounts are pulled into one shared inbox, so nothing gets missed across multiple platforms.

## WhatsApp automation
A business's WhatsApp number connects once. From then on, an AI assistant greets new contacts, answers common questions, and asks qualifying questions conversationally - not a rigid menu-based bot.

## AI lead qualification and scoring
As a conversation happens (on WhatsApp or elsewhere), the AI extracts useful details - what the person needs, their business type, budget, timeline, and whether they're the decision maker - and calculates a lead score. Higher-scoring leads are flagged as more sales-ready.

## CRM
Every lead, their conversation history, score, and status live in one CRM pipeline (from new contact through qualified, meeting scheduled, and won/lost) instead of being scattered across apps and spreadsheets.

## AI calling agent
For qualified leads who agree to it, an AI calling agent can place a first outreach call to confirm interest and details before a human salesperson gets involved.

## Meetings
Once a lead is ready, a meeting can be booked directly from the conversation.

## Human sales handoff
The AI's job is to qualify and warm up a lead - not to close the sale. Once a lead is qualified and ready, a real person from the sales team takes over the relationship. The AI never claims a deal is closed.
